Questions and answers

How do I start?

  1. Install Swipt on your computer (Windows PC or Mac) and open it.
  2. Install Swipt on your phone and open it.
  3. On the computer, click Pair a phone. On the phone, tap Pair with your computer and scan the QR code. Confirm on the computer.

That's it. From then on, pick photos or files on your phone and swipe up to send. You can also use Share ▸ Swipt in Photos or any other app.

My phone can't find my computer

  • Both must be on the same Wi-Fi. A phone on mobile data or a guest network can't reach your computer.
  • Swipt must be open on the computer (it can sit in the tray or menu bar with its window closed).
  • iPhone: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network ▸ Swipt must be on.
  • Windows: if Windows asked whether to allow Swipt on the network, it must be allowed. If Swipt shows "Phones can't reach this PC", follow what it says. Usually your Wi-Fi is set to Public and needs to be Private: Settings ▸ Network & internet ▸ Wi-Fi ▸ your network ▸ Private network.
  • Some routers keep devices apart ("client isolation" or "AP isolation", often on guest networks). Turn it off, or use your main network.

How do I send files from my computer to my phone?

Drag files onto the Swipt window on your computer (or click Send to phone) and choose your phone. They arrive as soon as Swipt is open on your phone.

Where do my files go?

  • Computer: Downloads ▸ Swipt (you can change it in Settings).
  • iPhone: photos and videos go to Photos; other files to the Files app ▸ On My iPhone ▸ Swipt.
  • Android: photos and videos go to your gallery (Pictures and Movies ▸ Swipt); other files to Downloads ▸ Swipt.

What do the free files mean?

Each phone can send or receive 100 files for free (one file is one transfer, whatever its size). After that, you can unlock unlimited transfers forever with one payment. No subscription, no ads, no account. The Swipt apps for Windows and Mac are free, and your phone's free files count for both directions.

I bought Unlimited Transfers. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Swipt ▸ Settings (the gear) ▸ Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Is it private?

Yes. Files go straight from your phone to your computer over your Wi-Fi, encrypted. They never touch the internet or any server, and Swipt collects no data. See the privacy policy.

How fast is it?

As fast as your Wi-Fi allows, often 10 to 30 MB per second at home. If the connection drops, Swipt picks up where it stopped.

How do I stop Swipt starting with my computer?

  • Windows: Swipt ▸ Settings ▸ "Start Swipt when you sign in to Windows".
  • Mac: Swipt ▸ Settings ▸ "Open Swipt when you log in to this Mac".

How do I remove a phone or start over?

  • On the computer: Swipt ▸ Phones to remove one phone, or Settings ▸ Start over ▸ Reset Swipt to remove all.
  • On the phone: Settings ▸ Reset Swipt.

Received files always stay where they are.
